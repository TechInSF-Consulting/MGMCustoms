Who we are

When the 1989 earthquake destroyed our office, the partners cleared shipments from their car phones. Four days later we were back at full strength.

Ted Mittelstaedt, Armando Galaviz and Gary Mylin founded MG&M in 1962 to give U.S. importers and exporters a high level of service. The business was rooted in meat and other perishables, where cargo has to keep moving and stay compliant with U.S. and foreign law.

All three founders have since passed away. Ted's son, Ed, leads the company today. Two of our people have been here more than thirty years each, and many of our clients have been with us just as long.

  1. 1957

    Ted Mittelstaedt earns customs broker license No. 3047.

  2. 1962

    MG&M opens in a one-room office on California Street, so crowded you climbed over desks to sit down.

  3. 1989

    The Loma Prieta quake levels 214 Front St. Back at full strength in four days.

  4. 2004

    Los Angeles office closes; every entry nationwide is now filed from San Francisco via Remote Location Filing.

  5. Today

    Ed Mittelstaedt leads a ten-person team at 341 Broadway.
