  1. Customs clearance

    Customs and Border Protection is the gateway for every import, and it wants more information earlier than ever. We work with CBP from pre-departure filings to release, so your goods clear without waiting at the port.

    • Importer Security Filing (ISF) and FDA Prior Notice
    • Entries filed in ACE, with deeper reporting on your supply chain
    • Partner agency clearances: FDA, USDA, FSIS, Fish & Wildlife
    • Any U.S. port, via Remote Location Filing from San Francisco
    • CTPAT validated member; SVI shared on request

  2. Freight forwarding

    As a licensed ocean intermediary (FMC No. 953R), we arrange shipping into and out of the U.S. by sea, and our agent partners clear and deliver at destination.

    • Ocean import and export: dry, temperature-controlled, containerized or project cargo
    • Export booking and documentation for steamship contract holders
    • Automated Export System (AES) filing
    • A partner network we've built since 1962

  3. Bonds & cargo insurance

    Every import needs a customs bond, and carriers limit their liability to protect themselves, not you. We arrange both.

    • Single-transaction, ISF and continuous customs bonds, with monitoring and reporting from our surety partner
    • Cargo insurance for air, sea, truck and rail through a top-rated cargo insurer
    • Rates negotiated on your commodity, claims history, modes and destinations

The hard-to-classify cargo is our specialty.

Some goods bring quotas, country restrictions and several agencies into a single entry. These are the ones we clear every week.

CheeseFDA · quota

Country restrictions, quotas, agricultural origin and food safety all meet on one entry. One of the hardest products to classify.

FSIS-regulated entries are where MG&M started. Ed Mittelstaedt ran an FSIS inspection facility before becoming a broker.

Fresh produceFDA · AMS

Seafood & wildlifeFWS
