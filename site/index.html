Who We Are

In 1962, Theodore Mittelstaedt, Armando Galaviz and Gary Mylin founded a Customs brokerage and freight forwarding company whose core belief was to provide US importers and exporters with a high degree of service.

With a business rooted in commodities such as meat and other perishables, there were many moving parts to keeping the cargo moving without delay and in compliance with US and foreign laws.

When the San Francisco earthquake struck in 1989, we kept working, even though our offices were demolished and the partners worked to clear shipments from mobile phones in their cars, all the while looking for new office space. Four days after the earthquake, we were back operating at full strength.

Today, all three founding partners have passed away, and Ted Mittelstaedt's son, Edward, continues to lead the company.

The son of founder Ted Mittelstaedt and a licensed Customs broker himself, Ed's earliest memories of the business are when his father would bring home thousands of cleared checks and his job would be matching carbons and putting them in order.

Key to our success are our people. Two of our employees have been with us for more than three decades each; two more have been with us for nearly a combined three decades themselves. That is experience that you cannot fabricate or pretend to have. Our customers and our employees have grown together, and for that we thank them both.
